📚 CS:GO Trade-Up Rules

1. Item Count Requirement

You need exactly 10 items from the same weapon and rarity tier to create a trade-up contract. Steam won't allow less or more.

2. Same Weapon & Rarity

All 10 items must be from the same weapon type (e.g., AK-47) AND same rarity tier (all Mil-Spec, or all Restricted, etc.).

3. Float Calculation

Formula: (wear1 + wear2 + ... + wear10) / 10 + 0.015 = expected float
The trade-up adds ~0.015 to the average float due to Steam's formula.

4. Wear Tolerance (±2%)

Steam allows a small variance in float values. Expected range: [avg_wear - 0.02, avg_wear + 0.02]

5. StatTrak Rules

Cannot mix StatTrak and normal skins in a contract. All 10 items must be either all StatTrak or all normal.

6. Possible Outcomes

Classified items have 3 possible outcomes (1/3 chance each): Tec-9 | Asiimov, MP9 | Vulcan, Aug | Magmatic.
